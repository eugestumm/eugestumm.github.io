I am Euge Stumm ([they/elle/elu](https://studentaffairs.howard.edu/diversity-inclusion/lgbtq-resources/pronouns)), a Ph.D. candidate in Literary, Cultural, and Linguistic Studies at the University of Miami. My research examines how gender and sexual dissidents in Latin America create language to encompass their experiences. My dissertation focuses on cultural productions in [gender-inclusive Spanish](https://www.genderinlanguage.com/spanish/how) in Argentina, and in [Pajubá](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pajubá) (or Bajubá), a Brazilian cryptolect. I am also a [digital humanist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_humanities) and a member of the [Bouchet Honor Society](https://gsas.yale.edu/edward-alexander-bouchet-graduate-honor-society).

## Digital projects

I build and maintain two digital archives:

- The [Bajubá Digital Archive](https://eugestumm.github.io/BajubaDigitalArchive/) catalogs cultural productions in and about Pajubá.
- The [Gender-Inclusive Spanish Digital Archive](https://eugestumm.github.io/gender-inclusive-spanish-digital-archive/) catalogs cultural productions in and about gender-inclusive Spanish.

I also develop [CollectionBuilder-Remix](https://github.com/eugestumm/CollectionBuilder-Remix), a community flavor of [CollectionBuilder](https://collectionbuilder.github.io/) designed for beginner-friendly and multilingual development of digital archives and collections, including from mobile devices. I am currently working to integrate it into the next generation of the CollectionBuilder ecosystem.
